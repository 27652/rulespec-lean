Generated Decimal expressions then replaced hand-written tests, preserving dependency order and rounding nodes, and passed 24 checks across four modes. Seven `min`, `max`, `ceil`, and `floor` checks passed. Numeric comparison conditions, judgment references, and versioned unindexed parameters passed six boundary cases and all 107 flat-tax cases. Indexed parameters passed 11 checks: five successful values and six invalid-index or missing-parameter faults. The 72 Decimal rule checks include missing versions, lazy evaluation, negative-zero numeric inputs, and 96-bit multiplication overflow. The runtime and generator share exact validation for 28-place values. Numeric agreement does not imply matching representation scale.
